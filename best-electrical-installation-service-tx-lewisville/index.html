A homeowner's preparation checklist

The installation process depends on the item and the existing setup, so share the facts you have and let the electrician assess the rest. Prepare a short list before calling:
- Identify the room or area where you want the work done.
- Explain what you want the finished installation to do.
- Say whether an existing item will be replaced or changed.
- Write down concerns about access, timing, or the current setup.

Don't try to remove a fixture, expose wiring, or test an unfamiliar electrical component to make the job easier to describe. Keep your explanation to what you can observe safely. If you see sparks, smell burning, or believe someone is in immediate danger, move away and contact emergency services as appropriate. For planned installation work, let an electrician handle electrical assessment and installation.
