Prepare a short project brief

Before arranging electrical work, gather the details you already know. You don’t need to diagnose a technical problem or make decisions about wiring. A simple brief can include:

  • The address and the room or area involved.
  • The item you want installed, if you have selected one.
  • What the equipment should do for your household.
  • Any related renovation plans or access instructions.
  • The main questions you want answered before work begins.

Avoid buying equipment or making changes based on guesses about compatibility. Don't remove covers or handle wiring to collect information. If a product has a model number or installation sheet, you can share those details instead. Ask what will happen if the electrician finds that the original request needs adjustment, and make sure you understand the next step before approving a change.
