Get the scope clear before work starts

An installation conversation should establish what you want changed and what the electrician will assess or install. The details vary by project, so ask for an explanation in terms you understand. You can use this short checklist:

  • Name the equipment or area involved, if known.
  • Explain what you want the finished result to do.
  • Share product details or renovation plans that affect the request.
  • Ask what is included, what may be outside the scope, and what decisions you need to make.
  • Find out whether permits or other approvals apply to your project.

Avoid choosing a plan based only on an assumption about what is behind a wall or what an existing electrical part can support. Don't attempt electrical work yourself if you’re unsure how to do it safely. If you notice a problem such as a burning smell, sparking, or a hot electrical component, stop using the affected equipment if you can do so safely and contact a qualified professional promptly.
