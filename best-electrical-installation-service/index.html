Questions to ask before choosing

Use a short checklist when you speak with an electrician. You don’t need technical terms to explain the project, but useful details can keep the conversation grounded.

  • What part of the home needs electrical work?
  • What do you want the finished installation to do?
  • Is the space being changed or updated at the same time?
  • What information about the equipment should you provide?
  • What should you expect before work can be scheduled?

Avoid selecting an installer based only on a quick description or assuming every electrical job is the same. Don’t leave out changes to the room, uncertainty about the existing equipment, or other details that could affect the request.
